I'm Muhammad Arham Khan a UI/UX Designer and Web Developer based in Karachi, Pakistan, currently pursuing a Bachelor's in Business and Information Technology at Virtual University and a Software Engineering programme at Aptech.

I believe great digital products are born at the intersection of precision engineering and empathetic design. My process begins with understanding people their goals, frustrations, and contexts before a single pixel is placed or a line of code is written.
